# SoNarr Landing Page Copy

## Product Name

SoNarr

## Brand Line

Narrative sonar for one-person finance desks.

## Product Positioning

SoNarr is a sonar for crypto narratives.

It detects emerging crypto market narratives from SoSoValue-powered data, news, and market signals, then turns them into publishable finance products: index ideas, research briefs, risk checks, and SoDEX-ready execution previews.

SoNarr is not another trading bot. It is a one-person narrative finance desk.

## Buildathon Context

SoNarr is being built for the SoSoValue Buildathon.

The buildathon theme is:

Build your one-person on-chain finance business with SoSoValue.

SoNarr should clearly show how one person can act like:
- a market analyst
- a research desk
- an index publisher
- a lightweight fund manager
- an agentic finance operator

## Wave 1 Scope

Wave 1 is a Web2 prototype.

Do not add smart contracts yet.
Do not add real trading yet.
Do not add wallet connection unless explicitly requested.
Do not overbuild execution.

The goal is to present a complete, polished product flow:
market signal → narrative detection → index idea → research brief → risk checks → execution preview → publishable product page.

## Problem Section

### Headline

Crypto moves through narratives. Most people see them too late.

### Body

Retail traders, analysts, and creators are flooded with news, token movements, social noise, and market rotations. By the time a trend becomes obvious, the best opportunity is often gone.

### Problem Cards

#### Scattered signals

News, prices, token flows, and sector movements live in different places.

#### Weak conviction

Most traders see a narrative but cannot explain why it matters.

#### No product layer

A good market insight rarely becomes a reusable index, report, or strategy.

#### Execution gap

Even when the idea is right, users still need risk checks and execution planning.

## Tone Rules

Use:
- clear SaaS language
- premium fintech tone
- confident product language
- simple explanations
- market structure language
- “one-person finance desk”
- “narrative radar”
- “publishable index”
- “research-to-execution”

Avoid:
- childish crypto language
- meme coin tone
- vague AI buzzwords
- overclaiming guaranteed returns
- saying the app makes users money
- saying it executes real trades in Wave 1
- saying it is decentralized if contracts are not implemented yet
- too many mentions of “AI” without explaining the workflow

## Important Positioning

Do not frame SoNarr as a basic chatbot.

Do not frame it as “user types thesis and AI generates portfolio.”

The stronger framing is:

SoNarr detects narratives first, then helps the user package them into finance products.

The product should feel proactive, like a radar/operator, not passive like a prompt box.
